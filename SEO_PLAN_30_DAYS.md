# SEO Plan: First 30 Days

## Week 1: Indexing and technical setup
- Connect Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster.
- Verify ownership for `https://medihairtour.ru/`.
- Submit `sitemap.xml` in both tools.
- Check indexing status and fix coverage issues.
- Ensure only one canonical URL version is indexed.

## Week 2: Semantic core and page targeting
- Build keyword clusters:
  - "пересадка волос сочи"
  - "пересадка волос пятигорск"
  - "пересадка бороды"
  - "пересадка бровей"
  - "метод fue пересадка волос"
- Map clusters to sections/pages.
- Add supporting FAQ questions for long-tail queries.

## Week 3: Content and trust signals
- Publish at least 2 expert articles:
  - Preparation for hair transplant
  - Recovery timeline after FUE
- Add more case studies in "до и после" with descriptive alt text.
- Strengthen E-E-A-T signals:
  - doctor expertise
  - clinic addresses in both cities
  - transparent treatment terms

## Week 4: Local SEO and authority
- Optimize Yandex/Google map business profiles for Sochi and Pyatigorsk.
- Request 10-20 real patient reviews.
- Add local citations in relevant medical/city directories.
- Track rankings for core keywords and adjust titles/descriptions.

## Weekly KPI checklist
- Indexed pages count
- Impressions and clicks (GSC/Yandex)
- Average position by core keyword groups
- Calls/forms from organic traffic
- CTR on branded vs non-branded queries
